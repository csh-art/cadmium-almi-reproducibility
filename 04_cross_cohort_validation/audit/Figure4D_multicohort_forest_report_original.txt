MANUSCRIPT CAPTION
Figure 4D. Cross-cohort effect estimates for the 11 prespecified hub genes. Points represent cohort-specific unshrunk log2 fold-changes and horizontal lines represent 95% Wald confidence intervals. Black points denote the GSE111016 discovery reference; blue and red points denote validation effects in the same and opposite directions, respectively. Grey crosses denote genes unavailable in the primary cohort-specific model. Double asterisks indicate same-direction evidence at targeted Benjamini-Hochberg FDR < 0.05 across the 11 prespecified genes, whereas single asterisks indicate same-direction nominal P < 0.05 without targeted-FDR significance. GSE111006 was interpreted as a same-SuperSeries small-cohort sensitivity analysis rather than independent external replication, and GSE167186 as an independent heterogeneous-phenotype sensitivity-validation cohort rather than exact-definition replication. 

METHODS TEXT
For each cohort, the unshrunk DESeq2 log2 fold-change and its Wald standard error were retained from the locked cohort-specific analysis. Ninety-five percent confidence intervals were calculated as log2 fold-change +/- 1.96 times the Wald standard error. Validation direction was evaluated relative to the locked GSE111016 discovery effect. Targeted multiple-testing correction used the Benjamini-Hochberg method with a fixed family size of 11 prespecified hub genes. No raw expression matrices were merged and no pooled meta-analytic estimate was calculated in this panel.
