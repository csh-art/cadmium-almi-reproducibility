# Study overview

The study integrates six evidence layers: NHANES epidemiology, CTD toxicogenomics, discovery skeletal-muscle transcriptomics, PPI/hub prioritization, cross-cohort mitochondrial pathway analyses, and exploratory gene-level MR.

Interpretation boundaries:
- NHANES is cross-sectional.
- GEO muscle cohorts do not contain measured cadmium exposure.
- Cross-layer convergence is prioritization, not proof of a single causal chain.
- Gene-level MR is not mediation of the cadmium–ALMI association.
